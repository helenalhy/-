---
name: Mall milestone decorations
description: Milestone rewards add themed decor to the existing twenty-floor mall without changing its themes or count.
---

Milestones may unlock free decorations that fit an existing floor's theme. Keep the current twenty floors, their themes, and their backgrounds unchanged; do not create replacement themes or extra floors as milestone rewards.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants progression rewards without altering the established mall themes.

**How to apply:** When adding progression rewards, add matching decor to an existing floor and confirm the reward does not replace a theme, change a background, or add a floor.
