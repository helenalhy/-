---
name: Motivation features and reward boundaries
description: Product rules for weekly challenges, dog companions, two-minute starts, snoozed reminders, and gentle restarts.
---

- Keep at most one active weekly challenge per user. Count unique check-in dates in the current local week, and do not set a target above the habit's scheduled practice days.
- The two-minute starter never completes a goal or grants coins automatically. If a habit has an explicitly configured minimum action, only the user's separate completion confirmation counts it as that day's goal check-in; the usual daily reward can be earned once.
- Generated minimum-action suggestions are hints, not saved habit edits; preserve user-written actions, and record the usual check-in only after explicit confirmation of the suggested step.
- Focus companions are free and separate from café staff ownership. A snooze is a one-time push for that habit and suppresses its recurring reminders for the local day without changing the saved daily schedule.
- Weekly challenge celebrations may animate mall visitors and decor, but they do not grant extra coins, change floor themes, or unlock new floors.
- Missed-day restart prompts should acknowledge previous effort and offer a small next action without shaming the user.

**Why:** The app's motivation features should support returning to habits without weakening its earned-coin economy or changing the user's mall.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries when extending minimum-action suggestions, habit challenges, timer behavior, mall reactions, or missed-day messaging.
