---
name: Weekly makeup check-in
description: One missed previous-day check-in may be recovered each calendar week and can earn its normal coin reward.
---

Allow one makeup of yesterday's missed, scheduled check-in per account per calendar week. A makeup can earn the normal check-in coins, but never pay twice for a date that already received its reward.

**Why:** The user wants a weekly chance to recover a missed check-in and receive coins for it.

**How to apply:** Keep the limit account-wide, preserve it even if the goal is later deleted, exclude planned rest days, and persist the recovery and coin reward through the recoverable check-in transaction.
